Art style options

The same little village in 5 styles. Villagers are much smaller in all of them (about a third of today's size). Tell me which one you like, or mix parts (e.g. "B's buildings with A's colors").

Today for comparison

The current look: big villagers, flat square roofs.

A · Soft Flat

Closest to today, but grown up. Hip roofs with four slopes and rounded eaves instead of flat squares, stone footings, little gardens and fences. Tiny villagers. Keeps the friendly mockup-E colors.

B · Roofless Pixel

The colony-sim look. Chunky pixel art and no roofs: you see inside every building (beds, tables, sacks of grain). Villagers are tiny 2-pixel people, so crowds read like an ant farm. Earthy, muted colors.

C · Storybook

Warm and painterly. Soft gradients and gentle blurred shadows, curvy thatched roofs, textured grass, rounder everything. Feels like a picture book. Villagers are small soft dots with a little light around them.

D · Tilted 3/4 View

Buildings with walls you can see. Still top-down, but slightly tilted so each building shows its front wall, windows and door under the roof. Villagers are tiny standing figures. Feels more like a living town, less like a map.

E · Ink & Wash

A hand-drawn map come alive. Parchment-colored ground, wobbly ink outlines, watercolor fills, hatched shadows. Villagers are tiny ink figures. Very readable, very calm.
